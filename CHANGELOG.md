# Changelog

All notable public Nomex Shift releases are recorded here. GitHub Releases contain the authoritative installer, checksum, release notes, and known issues for each published version.

## [2.3.0] - 2026-09-07

### Added

- Versioned EULA, Privacy Policy, Disclaimer, Acceptable Use, Premium, reseller, and third-party policy access.
- Required first-run legal acceptance and first-address-change safety notice.
- Minimal policy-acceptance records for Premium licensing.
- Shared Nomex licensing, signed product configuration, and signed update metadata.

### Changed

- Refined the compact Nomex Shift interface and product branding.
- Current legal-policy acceptance version is 1.1.

### Safety

- Core restore, recovery, Safe Mode, and diagnostics remain locally available.
- Shift remains a local adapter utility; it is not a VPN and does not provide anonymity.

Earlier internal builds were development iterations and are not automatically approved for public redistribution.
