# Frequently Asked Questions

## Is Nomex Shift a VPN?

No. It changes the address presented by a supported adapter on the local network. It does not hide your public IP address or route internet traffic through a private tunnel.

## Does Shift make me anonymous?

No. Networks, services, devices, accounts, browsers, and other identifiers may still identify or track activity.

## Will it work with every adapter?

No. Windows, the adapter, its driver, organizational policy, or network controls may reject or reverse an override.

## Why does connectivity briefly stop?

Windows normally has to restart or reconnect the selected adapter after changing its configured address.

## Can I restore the original address?

Use **Restore Original**. Safe Mode and Emergency Restore remain available for recovery. Preserve recovery information before changing an adapter.

## Is Premium required?

No for core inspection, address change/restore, recovery, and diagnostics. Premium enables additional features and requires server validation.

## What does Lifetime mean?

It means the supported lifetime of the applicable Nomex product or service, not the purchaser's lifetime or guaranteed permanent availability.

## Where is the source code?

The source is private. This repository intentionally contains only public release information, documentation, screenshots, checksums, and compiled release assets delivered through GitHub Releases.
