Premium is an optional license entitlement. It does not transfer ownership of Nomex Shift.

Depending on the current product configuration and entitlement, Premium may enable advanced generation modes, vendor-based addresses, profiles, identity slots, privacy sessions, startup automation, network memory, Nomex Guard, audit features, encrypted configuration backup, and appearance personalization.

Keys may have product, tier, duration, customer, order, and device limits. Activation sends the key, protected device identifier, and device label to the Nomex licensing service. A supported device can be deactivated from Shift; verified support can review recovery cases.

"Lifetime" means the supported lifetime of the applicable Nomex product or service—not the purchaser's lifetime and not guaranteed permanent availability.
