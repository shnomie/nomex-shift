# Nomex Acceptable Use Policy

**Version:** 1.1 · **Effective date:** September 7, 2026

You may use Nomex for legitimate personal privacy, testing on devices you own, authorized troubleshooting and administration, education, and research in authorized environments.

You may not use Nomex to perform or facilitate unauthorized access, fraud, ban evasion, impersonation, harassment, access-control circumvention, network interference, malware, malicious activity, infringement, violation of third-party terms, license or key abuse, unauthorized resale, distribution of modified builds, unwanted-software bundling, or misrepresentation as an official Nomex release.

Do not probe, scrape, overload, reverse engineer, automate abusive requests against, or attempt unauthorized access to Nomex APIs, licensing, updates, Discord bot, Admin Panel, payments, support, or infrastructure, except where applicable law prohibits the restriction or a written Nomex security-testing authorization applies.

Nomex may rate-limit, suspend, investigate, preserve relevant evidence, or terminate access for suspected violations, subject to applicable law. Report security issues privately using the process in [`SECURITY.md`](../SECURITY.md); do not publish exploitable details before a reasonable investigation period. Parental permission does not override laws, platform rules, age restrictions, contracts, or network policies.
