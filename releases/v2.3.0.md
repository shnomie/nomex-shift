# Nomex Shift v2.3.0

## Highlights

- Introduces the compact Nomex Shift product identity and focused Windows interface.
- Connects Premium entitlements, device management, signed configuration, and update checks to Nomex services.
- Adds versioned legal acceptance, local recovery safeguards, and privacy-conscious diagnostics.

## Changes

### Added

- Home, Spoofer, Profiles, Adapters, History, and Settings navigation.
- Restore Original, Emergency Restore, crash recovery, Safe Mode, and safety snapshots.
- Stable/Beta update channels with SHA-256 and signed release-metadata verification.
- Premium profiles, identity slots, privacy sessions, automation, network memory, Guard, audit, backup, and appearance capabilities when entitled.
- EULA, Privacy, Disclaimer, Acceptable Use, Premium, reseller, and third-party policy access.

### Changed

- Policy acceptance version is 1.1.
- Interface uses Nomex black, midnight-blue, electric-blue, and white branding.

## Known issues

- Some network adapters and drivers do not accept configurable MAC addresses.
- Windows or a driver may occasionally fail to restart an adapter automatically; use Restore Original, Safe Mode, or a Windows restart.
- An unsigned review artifact exists privately, but it must not be described as a signed production release.

## Safety

Nomex Shift changes the identity presented by an adapter on the local network. It does not hide a public IP address, replace a VPN, guarantee anonymity, or bypass network and platform rules.
